# iLocal V2 — targeted visual direction

Prepared before implementation. The requested direction authorizes the targeted changes below. GenSpark is visual authority; V1 and the supplied product documentation remain engineering and product authority. Reference exports are never imported into production.

## Evidence and composition

GenSpark sources: `../ilocal-website-2.0/genspark/iLocal Homepage.html`, extracted `css/base.css`, `css/home.css`, `js/home.js`, authentic legacy assets and supplied screenshots. V1 sources: current components/styles and deployed captures in `screenshots/`. Stitch remains the reference for authentic hardware and restrained enterprise messaging.

GenSpark uses Instrument Sans/Serif, 4.6vw section displays capped at 80px, approximately 17px body copy, 18–22px leads, 96–200px editorial section spacing, twelve-column asymmetric headings and approximately 300px narrative navigation beside its console. Its strongest differences are composition and information density, not font substitution. V1 already uses the right fonts but often uses small supporting copy, a centered stack, sparse panels and tiny audience diagrams. Restore the editorial relationships while retaining the approved V1 palette and headline rhythm.

## Brand cleanup and visual scope

Remove visible “Formerly iLocalBox” and old-brand strings from marketing/runtime metadata and unnecessary labels. Preserve migration/internal documentation. Authentic photographs retain physical legacy marks; replacement needs new approved photography, not retouching. Existing images `/hardware/main.png`, `expansion.png`, `high-density.png`, `refrigerated.png` require future brand review.

Counter, Curbside and Bedside illustrations gain spatial depth and scene composition; Courier and Mail Order receive genuinely different delivery/transit storytelling. Keep those changes limited to the existing illustration component. Never invent physical iLocal products.

## What remains unchanged

Next.js App Router, server-component defaults, typed content, supplied fonts/logo/palette, current navigation architecture, demo adapter/validation, patient journey behavior, animated platform routes, page URLs, SEO architecture and existing accessibility semantics. No new library, raw portal screenshot, PHI, telemetry, invented statistics or integration names. Cloud mock content is synthetic and explicitly labelled.
