# iLocal production website

## Source hierarchy

1. `../ilocal-website-2.0/ FINAL_DESIGN_SPEC.md` controls composition, copy, and interaction.
2. GenSpark supplies editorial rhythm, Instrument type, and route storytelling.
3. Stitch V4 supplies restraint and authentic hardware grounding. Its extra claims and dashboard styling are not adopted.
4. Supplied product requirements and regression plan constrain product claims. Configurable steps are not universal promises.

## Decisions

- Warm paper, royal blue sampled from official wordmark (#233f9f), navy, teal, cyan, selective gold. No borrowed Stitch gradients or telemetry styling.
- Only original supplied legacy hardware, with legacy branding preserved. No synthesized physical products.
- No approved human photos supplied: use clearly illustrative patient/staff journey diagrams, not fabricated customer imagery.
- Conceptual Cloud, phone and hardware configuration are labeled. Hardware selection is illustrative, not a validated bill of materials.
- No real patient data, metrics, testimonials, vendor badges or new certification claims.
- Self-host OFL Instrument fonts using official Fontsource distributions; retain licenses.
- RSC content by default. Client islands for navigation, routes, journey, Cloud, configuration, accordion and form.

## Open launch items

- Confirm public domain, customer-login URL, approved Privacy and Terms text.
- Select live demo destination/CRM. Local mock never sends or persists personal information. Production must fail closed until an adapter is configured.
- Confirm legal business name for Organization schema, final hardware availability and site-specific workflow scope.
- Review legacy URL inventory against supplied initial redirect matrix before domain cutover.
- No deployment is included without domain/hosting configuration.

Local implementation and verification are complete. See `VALIDATION.md` for measured results and explicit limits; SEO indexing, live CRM delivery and legal approval remain launch configuration rather than local build blockers.
